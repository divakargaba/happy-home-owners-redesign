Before you sign up

How does a snow pass work?

A pass covers your address for the whole winter, November to April. Whenever 2 cm or more falls, we clear within 24 hours of the snow stopping. You pay one flat price, whether it snows five times or fifty.

What if I sign up after winter has started?

Service starts right away and runs to April 30.

How will I know you have been?

You get a text or email after each visit, and another if we come back for an end-of-storm check.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Paying in full up front gives the biggest saving, and monthly payments are also available.

Can I cancel partway through?

No. Passes cannot be cancelled or refunded, so ask us anything you are unsure about before you book.

Are you insured?

Yes, fully.
